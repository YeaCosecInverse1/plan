# 09 — JAPAN ARRIVAL PLAN

## QUICK START

The first Japanese months are about **stability first, optimization second**.

### First priorities

1. Immigration/residence procedures required after arrival.
2. Address/residence registration where required.
3. Health insurance/social insurance arrangements.
4. Bank/payment setup.
5. Phone/internet.
6. Housing.
7. Commuting.
8. Employer onboarding.
9. Daily-life routine.
10. Japanese workplace adaptation.

The exact procedures depend on my residence status and municipality.

## FIRST WEEK

- Complete required immigration/local registration steps.
- Confirm accommodation.
- Set up essential communications/payment.
- Learn commute.
- Learn nearby grocery/pharmacy/clinic.
- Save emergency numbers and employer contacts.
- Do not overload myself with sightseeing/admin simultaneously.

## FIRST MONTH

Understand:

- workplace rules
- attendance
- communication norms
- documentation
- incident escalation
- security requirements
- payroll
- benefits
- leave
- insurance
- pension
- tax procedures

Keep copies of important confirmations.

## FIRST THREE MONTHS AT WORK

My goal is not to redesign everything.

I should:

- understand the systems
- understand the team
- deliver reliably
- document my work
- ask precise questions
- learn Japanese used by my team
- identify one useful improvement

## JAPANESE

Every day, connect language study to actual life:

- train announcements
- workplace phrases
- menus
- forms
- internal terminology
- technical documentation
- polite requests

JLPT is useful evidence, but real workplace comprehension is the goal.

## CAREER

I should preserve the engineering trajectory I built in Europe.

Track:

- responsibilities
- systems
- technologies
- incidents
- improvements
- measurable outcomes

Do not mentally reset to junior.

## YEAR-ONE GATE

I want:

- stable residence
- stable employment
- clean tax/insurance/pension compliance
- stronger Japanese
- documented professional achievements
- updated HSP calculation
- emergency savings rebuilding
