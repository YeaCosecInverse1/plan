# 10 — JAPAN PR PLAN

## QUICK START

PR is a legal process, not a reward for simply living in Japan for a certain number of years.

My job is to keep a strong career profile **and** keep the boring administrative parts clean.

### Every year

- verify the current PR rules
- verify my HSP category/points
- keep employment evidence
- keep tax records
- keep insurance/pension records
- keep residence records
- keep Japanese-language evidence
- review income/career progression
- update the document archive

## HSP FAST-TRACK CONCEPT

Japan has a Highly Skilled Professional framework and special PR pathways for qualifying highly skilled professionals. The commonly cited framework has included a shorter PR residence-period route for people meeting the relevant points threshold, but the exact requirements and calculation must be checked against the current official rules when I apply.

I therefore do **not** treat “80 points = automatic PR” as a rule.

## SCORE SHEET

Keep two numbers:

### Conservative

Only count points for which I have strong evidence and clear current eligibility.

### Scenario

Include plausible points that still need verification.

Never make a life decision from the optimistic number alone.

## EVIDENCE

Keep organized records for:

- passport
- residence cards/status
- degree
- university qualification evidence where relevant
- employment
- experience
- income
- tax
- pension
- health insurance
- Japanese qualifications
- professional certifications
- other qualifying evidence

## PUBLIC OBLIGATIONS

Do not treat taxes, insurance or pension as paperwork that can be fixed at the end.

Keep them current and retain evidence.

## APPLICATION CHECK

Before filing:

- current official PR guideline checked
- current HSP rules checked
- points recalculated
- all evidence matched to each claimed point
- residence history checked
- tax/insurance/pension records checked
- required forms/documents checked
- employment and income situation checked
- professional references/evidence organized

## TIMELINE

My original target is PR before 29.

That is a target, not a promise.

If the actual legal requirements, career timing or residence history make that impossible, I do not destroy the career just to preserve the date. I update the plan.
