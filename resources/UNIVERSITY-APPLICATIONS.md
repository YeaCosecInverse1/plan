# ADD-ON — UNIVERSITY APPLICATION SYSTEM

## QUICK START

I do not choose a university from one ranking number.

I compare the whole route.

## MATRIX

For every candidate record:

- country
- university
- programme
- degree duration
- language
- tuition
- living cost
- scholarships
- admission requirements
- English requirement
- application deadline
- visa/proof-of-funds requirements
- internship/job market
- international support
- curriculum
- current HSP university-category relevance

## APPLICATION TIERS

Use:

- ambitious
- realistic
- safer

Do not put every application into one basket.

## VERIFICATION

For every important fact, save:

- official URL
- date checked
- exact requirement
- notes

University pages beat old social-media posts.

Immigration authority pages beat forum summaries.

## BEFORE SUBMITTING

Check:

- name spelling
- passport information
- grades
- transcripts
- translations
- references
- English test
- financial documents
- deadlines
- programme-specific requirements
