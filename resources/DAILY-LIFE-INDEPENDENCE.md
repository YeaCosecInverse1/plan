# ADD-ON — DAILY LIFE / INDEPENDENCE

## QUICK START

Before Europe I should be able to run my own life without someone quietly fixing everything behind me.

## FOOD

Learn:

- 5–10 cheap meals
- grocery planning
- food storage
- expiry dates
- basic nutrition
- cooking safely
- cleaning while cooking

## HOME

Learn:

- laundry
- cleaning
- bathroom/kitchen hygiene
- waste separation rules
- minor household troubleshooting
- how to contact landlord/maintenance

## MONEY

Learn:

- monthly budgeting
- bank transfers
- card security
- exchange rates
- subscriptions
- emergency funds
- receipts/invoices
- avoiding unnecessary debt

## ADMIN

Be able to:

- make appointments
- fill forms
- read official messages
- keep deadlines
- store documents
- contact university/employer/landlord

## TRANSPORT

Practice:

- route planning
- public transport
- late-night alternatives
- airport travel
- reading signs
- emergency routes

## EMERGENCIES

Know:

- local emergency number
- police/fire/ambulance procedures
- embassy/consular contact
- university emergency contact
- employer emergency contact
- insurance contact

The exact numbers and procedures depend on the country.
