# ADD-ON — MONEY / FREELANCE

## QUICK START

I do not build the whole career around freelancing.

The order is:

**skill → proof → useful outcome → reliable delivery → reputation → better work**

## WHEN TO START

I start offering services after I can deliver one small outcome independently.

Possible early services:

- Python automation
- CSV/JSON processing
- API integrations
- small scripts
- simple backend fixes

Later:

- Dockerization
- Linux deployment
- cloud deployment
- CI/CD
- Terraform
- monitoring

## WHAT I AM BUYING WITH EARLY INCOME

Early legitimate income should strengthen:

- emergency reserve
- education/departure fund
- application costs
- technical equipment
- Japan relocation reserve

## WHAT I DO NOT DO

I do not:

- promise work I cannot deliver
- copy a tutorial and sell it as expertise
- take on high-risk production systems before I understand them
- expose a client's secrets
- let freelancing destroy university or core engineering learning

## MILESTONES

I treat income milestones as observations, not guarantees.

**First paid task:** proof that somebody valued the outcome.

**Repeat client:** proof that I can deliver reliably.

**Higher-value work:** proof that my capability and responsibility have increased.

## EUROPE

Once I have legal student/work permission, local employment experience may be more strategically valuable than maximizing freelance volume.

## JAPAN

Freelancing is not part of the immigration plan unless a specific lawful status and tax situation clearly support it.

Check current rules before doing side work.
