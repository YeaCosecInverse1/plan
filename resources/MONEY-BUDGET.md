# ADD-ON — MONEY / BUDGET

## QUICK START

I separate money into:

1. required
2. useful
3. optional

I never build the education plan around an income number that has not been verified.

## PRE-EUROPE

Budget:

- applications
- tests
- visa
- tuition/deposit
- flight
- housing
- first month
- emergency reserve

## EUROPE STUDENT

Track real monthly spending.

After 1–2 months, calculate:

fixed costs + food + transport + study + health + discretionary.

Then set a realistic monthly budget.

## EUROPE JOB

Build:

- emergency fund
- relocation fund
- Japan fund
- normal savings

Do not assume gross salary equals spendable salary. Taxes and social contributions vary by country.

## JAPAN

Before moving, calculate a separate arrival budget.

After arrival, rebuild emergency savings before aggressive lifestyle spending.

## RULE

If a number concerns tuition, tax, salary, visa funds, work hours, insurance or immigration, I verify the exact country, date and official source before treating it as fact.
