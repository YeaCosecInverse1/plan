# 06 — EUROPE YEAR 4, IF MY DEGREE NEEDS IT

## QUICK START

This file exists because not every Bachelor's takes exactly three years.

If my programme is three years, I do not invent a fourth year just because the roadmap has a Year 4 file.

If it is four years, Year 4 is for **graduation + professional launch**, not for starting another giant learning roadmap.

## PRIORITIES

1. Finish the degree strongly.
2. Keep working if the job/internship is valuable and compatible with study.
3. Convert internship/working-student experience into full-time employment where sensible.
4. Complete portfolio evidence.
5. Apply broadly to relevant engineering roles.
6. Prepare immigration/work transition according to the exact country.
7. Continue Japanese.
