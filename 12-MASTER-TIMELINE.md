# 12 — MASTER TIMELINE

## QUICK START

This is the calendar view.

I should not use it to micromanage my life. I use it to answer:

**“Which phase am I supposed to be in, and what must come out of it?”**

The dates are targets. University length, admission timing, employment and immigration rules can move them.

### Main job

Prepare for Europe.

### Technical

Computer fundamentals → Linux → Git → Python → networking → SQL/HTTP → FastAPI/PostgreSQL → Docker when ready.

### English

IELTS + general/technical English.

### Japanese

Small consistent habit.

### Life

Cooking, budgeting, documents, banking, backups, travel, personal admin.

### Exit condition

I have a realistic university route, the required English/test preparation, departure finances/documents, and a usable technical foundation.

### Career

### Language

### Career

### Main job

### Technical

### Career

Stronger internship/working-student role and graduate-job applications.

I have credible project evidence, relevant work experience and a clear job story.

## EUROPE YEAR 4, IF NEEDED

- current HSP scenario calculation

### First priority

Apply when the real requirements are satisfied, not when the calendar says I should.

I stop optimizing every decision around immigration and start optimizing more around:

**career + money + family + health + life.**
