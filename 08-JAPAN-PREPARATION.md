# 08 — PREPARING TO MOVE TO JAPAN

## QUICK START

I do not move to Japan because I like Japan.

I move when the **job, immigration status, finances and long-term career path** make sense together.

### Before accepting an offer

Verify:

- job duties
- employment type
- compensation
- probation
- working hours
- overtime rules/pay
- location
- relocation support
- health/social insurance arrangements
- residence status pathway
- start date
- language expectations
- career progression
- whether the role actually uses my engineering skills

## 12–24 MONTH PREPARATION

### Technical

Keep improving the production skills I actually use:

- Linux
- networking
- cloud
- Docker
- Terraform
- CI/CD
- observability
- security
- Kubernetes
- automation

### Japanese

Move from exam preparation toward workplace Japanese:

- technical vocabulary
- meetings
- polite speech
- email/chat
- incident communication
- reading internal documentation
- explaining technical problems

### Documents

Prepare a clean evidence folder:

- passport
- degree
- transcript
- employment certificates
- experience records
- payslips/tax evidence where relevant
- Japanese certificates
- CV
- references
- identity/residence records
- required immigration documents

## HSP

Use the current official points table at the time I apply.

Build:

- conservative score
- optimistic score
- evidence for each point
- missing evidence
- expiry/validity notes

A third-party calculator can help me model scenarios, but it does not decide eligibility.

## MONEY

Build a Japan arrival budget for:

- flight
- temporary accommodation
- deposit/key money where applicable
- first rent
- utilities
- food
- transport
- furniture/household basics
- phone/internet
- emergency reserve

Do not spend the entire savings buffer on the move.

## FINAL 30 DAYS

- confirm flight
- confirm accommodation
- confirm employer instructions
- back up documents
- inform relevant authorities/banks as needed
- obtain required cash/payment methods
- make an emergency contact sheet
- reduce possessions
- keep critical documents in carry-on

## FINAL GATE

I should be able to explain:

- why this Japanese job is relevant
- what residence status I am using
- what I need to do after arrival
- how much money I have
- where I will live
- how I get to work
- what happens if employment changes
- what my HSP/PR plan currently is
- what I need to verify again before filing anything
